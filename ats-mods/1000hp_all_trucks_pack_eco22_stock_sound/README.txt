ATS 1000 HP / 4500 Nm engine pack — Stock Sound Edition

All 18 supplied engine definitions now use the STOCK Kenworth W900 audio
through SCS FMOD events (engine, exhaust, start_bad, turbo, remote).
They reuse game-installed sound banks; no copyrighted audio banks are
redistributed with this mod.

Tuning unchanged from previous revision:
- consumption_coef: 0.22 (18 US MPG is a target, not a verified result)
- 1,000–2,000 RPM acceleration-focused torque curve
- 4,500 Nm torque and existing RPM/shift settings

Install:
1. Place 1000hp_all_trucks_pack_eco22_stock_sound.scs in your
   American Truck Simulator/mod directory.
2. Disable/remove ALL previous revisions of this 1000HP engine pack.
3. Enable this package in Mod Manager; reselect the 1000HP engine at
   a service station if audio doesn't immediately return.
4. Check Documents/American Truck Simulator/game.log.txt for missing bank
   or soundref errors if it stays silent.

Note:
All trucks will use the SAME vanilla Kenworth W900 engine sound,
not their manufacturer-specific sounds. Sound bank paths are based on
published vanilla configuration examples and are not verified against
an installed copy of ATS 1.61.
The original supplied truck folder identifiers are preserved exactly.
